# Credora AI: Recruiter Intelligence Operating System
## Project Scope & Screen List

The goal is to create a seamless, cinematic journey that feels like a single living system evaluating the user.

### 1. Neural Intro Page
Fullscreen cinematic animation. Neural pathway reveal and "CREDORA AI" scan-wave logo.

### 2. Landing / Hero Experience
"Decode recruiter trust." Ambient motion, floating intelligence overlays, and the "START NEURAL ANALYSIS" entry point.

### 3. Login / Auth Experience
Classified intelligence access. Cinematic Google OAuth and secure login flow.

### 4. Resume Intelligence Scan
The resume dissolves into data. ATS scan beams, extraction particles, and impact detection visuals.

### 5. Recruiter Attention Heatmap™
Simulation of recruiter eye-tracking. Gaze trails, hotspots, and trust-drop zones.

### 6. GitHub Intelligence Constellation™
Neural repository links. Floating constellations of engineering maturity and deployment proof.

### 7. Tutorial Project Detection™
Anomaly detection UI. Similarity heatmaps and architecture fingerprinting to detect generic projects.

### 8. Communication Pulse™
Voice and waveform analysis. Speaking confidence meter and recruiter impression orb.

### 9. Adaptive Technical Pulse™
Kinetic technical questioning. Real-time recruiter trust adjustment based on answers.

### 10. Confidence vs Evidence Engine™ (Signature Experience)
A collision of streams. Visualizing the alignment (harmony) or mismatch (instability) of candidate claims.

### 11. Recruiter Brain Simulation™
Floating subconscious thoughts. Fading psychological insights into recruiter hesitation.

### 12. Silent Rejection Risk Engine™
Hidden signals. Visualizing invisible risks and authenticity instability.

### 13. Recruitability Dashboard™
The final intelligence artifact. Radar graphs, Recruitability Index™, and hiring probability.

### 14. Improvement Simulator™
Future growth trajectories. Quantified metrics to improve the "Intelligence score."

### 15. Startup vs MNC Fit Engine™
Compatibility visualization. shortlisting probability based on engineering-team culture.
